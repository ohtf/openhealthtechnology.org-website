# Advisers

OHTF's advisers bring clinical, technical and specialist expertise to the software we look after, so that it is built with the people who will rely on it. They advise OHTF and its maintainers; decisions stay with the directors and with each project's maintainers.

OHTF's founders also serve as advisers. That role is separate from their position as directors of Open Health Technology CIC, which is set out under [governance](../governance/index.md#directors-and-members).
