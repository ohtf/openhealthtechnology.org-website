# Governance

OHTF's governance is meant to be clear, proportionate and public. Day-to-day project decisions are made by the people doing the work, within explicit, public rules: see [do-ocracy](do-ocracy.md). This section records how decisions are made, what legal form OHTF is taking, and the commitments that bind it.

## Current status

!!! warning "Not yet incorporated"

    OHTF is **preparing to incorporate** as **Open Health Technology Foundation CIC**. It is not yet a registered company, cannot yet enter into contracts in its own name, and does not yet hold assets. This page will be updated with the company and CIC registration details once incorporation is complete.

Until incorporation, OHTF operates as an open working group around the [github.com/ohtf](https://github.com/ohtf) organisation. Projects that join during this period keep their existing licences and copyright holders.

## Legal form

OHTF will be a UK **community interest company (CIC) limited by guarantee**.

- **Limited by guarantee.** There are no shareholders and no dividends. Each legal member's liability is limited to £1.
- **Community interest company.** OHTF must operate for the benefit of the community, reports annually to the CIC Regulator, and is subject to a statutory **asset lock**: its assets cannot be distributed for private gain and can only be transferred for community benefit.
- **Can trade.** A CIC can charge for services, contract commercially, employ people and receive grants. That is how OHTF will pay for maintenance without enclosing the code.

We chose a CIC over a charity because it allows the technical and commercial agility a young foundation needs, while still giving a credible, locked-in public-benefit commitment. We chose it over a plain company limited by guarantee because the asset lock and CIC status are a clear public signal to health-service and public-sector partners.

## Objects

The draft objects of the company are:

> to carry on activities which benefit the community and, in particular, to advance health and healthcare for the public benefit by providing a neutral, sustainable and open-source home for reusable health technology.

In furtherance of that, OHTF may develop, maintain, publish, host, steward and support software, standards, technical documentation and other shared digital infrastructure that promotes safe, effective, equitable and interoperable healthcare, and support the people and organisations who use or maintain it.

## Constitution

OHTF's Articles of Association adapt the CIC Regulator's model Articles for a small-membership company limited by guarantee, with an added, entrenched [open-source commitment](open-source-commitment.md). The draft Articles are undergoing independent legal review and will be published here once filed.

## Directors and members

OHTF will have a board of directors, who are also its legal company members, as the CIC Regulator's small-membership model provides. We intend to add independent non-executive directors after incorporation. Directors' names will be listed here once appointed and registered at Companies House.

Directors record conflicts of interest and related-party transactions, and take OHTF's legal decisions collectively.

## Public membership

OHTF may later offer an annual public **OHTF Membership** for individuals and organisations who want to support the foundation and access shared resources. Public membership is a subscription. It does not make you a legal member of the company, and it does not give voting rights, directorship or management authority.
