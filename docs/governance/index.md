# Governance

OHTF's governance is meant to be clear, proportionate and public. Day-to-day project decisions are made by the people doing the work, within explicit, public rules: see [do-ocracy](do-ocracy.md). This section records how decisions are made, OHTF's legal form, and the commitments that bind it.

## Legal form

OHTF is a UK **community interest company (CIC) limited by guarantee**.

- **Limited by guarantee.** There are no shareholders and no dividends. Each legal member's liability is limited to £1.
- **Community interest company.** OHTF must operate for the benefit of the community, reports annually to the CIC Regulator, and is subject to a statutory **asset lock**: its assets cannot be distributed for private gain and can only be transferred for community benefit.
- **Can trade.** A CIC can charge for services, contract commercially, employ people and receive grants. That is how OHTF pays for maintenance without enclosing the code.

We chose a CIC over a charity because it allows the technical and commercial agility a foundation needs, while still giving a credible, locked-in public-benefit commitment. We chose it over a plain company limited by guarantee because the asset lock and CIC status are a clear public signal to health-service and public-sector partners.

## Objects

The objects of the company, from Article 5 of its [Articles of Association](../transparency/articles-of-association.md), are:

> to carry on activities which benefit the community and, in particular, to advance health and healthcare for the public benefit by providing a neutral, sustainable and open-source home for health technology, from focused libraries to national-scale clinical software services.

In furtherance of that, the company may develop, maintain, publish, host, steward and support software, standards, technical documentation and other shared digital infrastructure; provide technical stewardship and operational support for clinical digital tools; provide clinical safety, regulatory and governance infrastructure; and support the people and organisations who use or maintain them.

## Constitution

OHTF's Articles of Association adapt the CIC Regulator's model Articles for a small-membership company limited by guarantee, with an added, entrenched [open-source commitment](open-source-commitment.md). The Articles are [published in full](../transparency/articles-of-association.md).
